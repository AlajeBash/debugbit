Get Started In Under 2 Minutes

Follow these quick steps to load the unpacked extension into Google Chrome.

1

Download Release ZIP

Grab the latest pre-built distribution archive of AI Debugging Copilot from our official repository releases page.

2

Load Unpacked Extension

Navigate to chrome://extensions/, enable Developer mode in the top-right, and select "Load unpacked" pointing to the zip's dist folder.

3

Input Gemini Key & Debug

Open Chrome DevTools (F12), navigate to the AI Debugging Copilot panel, paste your personal Gemini API key, and click Record!

Local-First Privacy Manifesto

We believe your code, network requests, and developer telemetry should belong solely to you.

Zero Central Telemetry Database

We do not operate a remote server, metrics broker, or tracking endpoint. All session records are saved exclusively in your browser’s sandboxed IndexedDB database.

Direct, Serverless API Connection

Diagnostic requests are transmitted directly as a standard POST payload from your local client to Google's official Gemini REST endpoints, authenticated by your personal key. No third-party servers intercept this channel.

Complete User Control

Telemetry captures are active only when the iOS-style recording switch is enabled. You can wipe, clear, or export your local database logs instantly at any moment with a single click.

Open Source Auditable Codebase

Our complete code is published openly on GitHub, allowing you to audit, review, and verify that our client-side pipelines meet your corporate or private security standards.
